About Zendesp

A Preston-based financial information consulting firm dedicated to transforming complexity into clarity for decision-makers across the UK and international markets.

Our Story

Built on Integrity & Insight

Founded in 2009, Zendesp began as a boutique research practice serving Lancashire-based investment firms. Today, we serve over 500 clients across 12 global markets, maintaining the same commitment to accuracy and personalised service that defined our origins.

Our multidisciplinary team includes chartered financial analysts, regulatory specialists, and sector researchers who collaborate to deliver holistic advisory packages — never siloed reports.

Our Consulting Framework

We begin every engagement with a structured discovery session to understand your objectives, constraints, and success criteria. No obligation initial consultation.

Deliverables include a scoped project brief, timeline, and fee proposal tailored to your budget and urgency.

Our analysts aggregate data from Bloomberg, Refinitiv, company filings, and proprietary databases. All sources are cross-verified before analysis.

We maintain full audit trails so you can trace every figure back to its origin — essential for board-level presentations.
