Who We Are

About Zendesk

Founded on the belief that every customer interaction matters, Zendesk has grown into the world's most trusted customer experience platform — empowering teams to build lasting relationships at scale.

Our mission is to help companies of all sizes create better customer relationships. From startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, organizations rely on Zendesk to centralize support channels, automate routine tasks, and gain actionable insights that drive satisfaction and loyalty.

Headquartered with a strong presence across Europe and beyond, we combine cutting-edge AI with human-centered design to deliver support experiences that feel personal, even at global scale.
